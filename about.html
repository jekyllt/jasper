Introduction

Hi, I'm Aldi Saputra Wahyudi, widely known online as Aldisaw. I'm a cybersecurity professional with over five years of experience in penetration testing, application security, and vulnerability research. I enjoy uncovering complex security flaws, helping teams build resilient software, and creating automation that makes security workflows faster and more reliable.

Professional Background & Academic Journey

My work sits at the intersection of offensive security and software engineering. Over the past five-plus years, I have focused on identifying vulnerabilities across web applications, APIs, and mobile platforms, as well as conducting independent vulnerability research and responsible disclosure.

Earlier in my journey, I participated in the Born to Protect Challenge (2018) under the mentorship of Raditya Iryandi—an experience that shaped how I communicate technical security concepts clearly. I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Informatics while expanding my research into cloud security, digital forensics, secure software engineering, and AI-powered automation.

Technical Expertise

  • Application & API Security: Threat modeling, OWASP Top 10 assessments, authentication & authorization testing, and secure code review.
  • Mobile Application Security: Android penetration testing, reverse engineering, dynamic runtime hooking (Frida, Magisk, Zygisk, JsHook), and OWASP MASVS/MASTG.
  • Vulnerability Research & Bug Bounty: Independent security research, root-cause analysis, and coordinated disclosure (including CVE-2023-27636).
  • AI & Security Automation: Building Python tooling and experimenting with AI agents to automate security testing and repetitive engineering tasks.

Creative Background & Personal Interests

Beyond cybersecurity, I have a background in digital content creation and storytelling. Earlier on, I also spent time experimenting with creative digital media and interactive experiences. While my primary focus today is cybersecurity and software engineering, that creative foundation continues to shape how I write technical articles, design presentations, and share ideas.

Outside of work, I enjoy traveling, backpacking, photography, and documenting personal observations on my Blog. I believe technical depth and personal curiosity complement each other—and knowledge becomes far more valuable when shared openly.
